# grilling (skill)

Stage 2 of the pipeline: a relentless interview that sharpens an idea, plan, or
design into shared understanding, walking the decision tree one question at a
time with a recommended answer for each.

- `SKILL.md` - the interview contract and its trigger phrases (grill, shape
  this, stress-test this).

Adapted from Matt Pocock's `productivity/grilling` skill (MIT; license
preserved at `../wayfinder/LICENSE.upstream`).
